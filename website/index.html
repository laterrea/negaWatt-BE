Context

Sufficiency first, then efficiency, then renewables

The négaWatt method deliberately prioritises sufficiency — avoiding waste and changing behaviour — before efficiency (delivering the same service with less energy) and only then scaling renewables. The reference year is 2019, chosen to avoid COVID distortions; the horizon is 2050, with linear trajectories between milestone years.
