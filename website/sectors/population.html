Population & context

Every sector model rests on shared macro assumptions: how many people live in Belgium, how households are formed, and the physical conversion factors that translate activity into energy. These context levers do not belong to a single end-use sector, but they shape the denominator of almost every per-capita or per-household indicator on this site.
