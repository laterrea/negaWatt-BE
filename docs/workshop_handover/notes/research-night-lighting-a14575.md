## Overview

Commission Regulation (EU) 2019/2024 of 1 October 2019 lays down ecodesign requirements for refrigerating appliances with a direct sales function pursuant to Directive 2009/125/EC.

## Scope and Application

The Regulation establishes ecodesign requirements for the placing on the market or the putting into service of electric mains-operated refrigerating appliances with a direct sales function, including appliances sold for refrigeration of items other than foodstuffs. Rules on Ecodesign for refrigerating appliances with a direct sales function apply from 1 March 2021 under Regulation (EU) 2019/2024.

## Energy Efficiency Requirements

EU energy labels for refrigerating appliances with a direct sales function use, as of 1 March 2021, a scale from A (most efficient) to G (least efficient) under Regulation (EU) 2019/2018. ## Environmental Impact

Refrigerating appliances with a direct sales function is one of the product groups with an estimated 48 TWh of annual final energy savings in 2030.

## Key Regulation: Decree 2022-1295

The decree n°2022-1295 of October 5, 2022 requires that openings of buildings or parts of buildings used for tertiary activities, whether commercial or non-commercial, that are heated or cooled with heating or air conditioning systems must be equipped with manual or automatic closure systems limiting thermal losses.

When one or more of these heating or air conditioning systems are operating, these closure systems must be kept closed, including during hours when the premises are open to the public.

## Enforcement and Penalties

Mayors and prefects have the ability to directly sanction infractions, with fines that can reach 750 euros.

## Energy Savings Benefits

Regarding refrigerated display cases specifically, closing refrigerated doors can result in energy savings of 20% for single glazing and 50% for double glazing.

This regulation is part of France's broader climate and energy resilience measures, aiming to reduce unnecessary energy consumption in commercial establishments through mandatory door closure when heating or cooling systems are in operation.
