## Highway Lighting Changes in 2011

Since July 15, 2011, a new regulation has applied in the Flemish Region. Many motorways in Belgian Flanders are no longer lit, with lighting remaining only on major ring roads and on- and off-ramps.

Previously, lights on motorways were switched off between midnight and 6 a.m., but with the new measure, the lighting disappeared completely. However, the regulation wasn't quite permanent darkness: In September, when it was still dark at 6 a.m., Belgium switched the lights back on.

## Exceptions to the New Policy

Lighting remains on the on- and off-ramps of motorways, on the rings of Antwerp, Ghent and Brussels, on the winding E314 in Leuven, and on road sections where the distance between on- and off-ramps is shorter than 3 km. In some places, 'dynamic lighting' has been installed, where lights can be activated in exceptional circumstances such as traffic accidents, road works, traffic jams, or extreme weather conditions.

## Energy and Environmental Benefits

The Belgians wanted to save energy and limit light pollution with this measure. The regulation was expected to save about 2 million euros per year.
